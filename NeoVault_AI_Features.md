# NeoVault: Advanced AI & Machine Learning Features

## 1. Smart Financial Insights (AI Advisor)
* **Predictive Balances**: Uses time-series forecasting (like ARIMA or Prophet) to predict the user's account balance at the end of the month based on historical spending and upcoming recurring bills.
* **Auto-Categorization**: Employs Natural Language Processing (NLP) to automatically categorize transactions based on the merchant name (e.g., mapping "UberEats" to "Dining" and "Netflix" to "Entertainment"), eliminating manual categorization.
* **Personalized Summaries**: Integrates a Large Language Model (LLM) to generate weekly human-readable financial summaries, providing contextual insights on spending habits.

## 2. Intelligent Anomaly & Fraud Detection
* **Fraud Alerts**: Implements machine learning isolation models to detect outliers. If an unusual spending pattern is recognized, the system triggers real-time critical warnings.
* **Duplicate Bill Warning**: Scans recurring payments and bills using AI to alert users if there is a high probability of paying the same invoice twice.
* **Network Graph Fraud Detection**: Analyzes a massive graph of user and account interactions in real-time to flag transactions involving known fraudulent nodes or suspicious payment chains.

## 3. Advanced Personalization & Wealth Management
* **Robo-Advisory (Algorithmic Trading)**: An AI system that automatically invests a user's spare change or allocates their portfolio based on risk tolerance, age, and financial goals.
* **Dynamic Budget Generation**: Analyzes the past 12 months of spending, adjusts for inflation and local pricing trends, and suggests an optimal, dynamic monthly budget tailored to the user's evolving lifestyle.
* **Predictive Cash Flow Modeling**: Provides a calendar view predicting exactly when a user might face a cash shortage based on upcoming auto-pays and average spending velocity.
* **Next-Best-Action (NBA) Recommendations**: Determines the single most valuable financial action a user should take right now, such as paying off high-interest debt or moving funds to a high-yield savings account.

## 4. Hyper-Advanced Security & Authentication
* **Biometric Behavioral Analysis**: Silently learns how the user interacts with their device (screen pressure, holding angle, typing speed). It blocks logins if the physical interaction pattern deviates from the true user's baseline.
* **Voice Biometrics**: Authenticates high-value transactions using a short voice sample by analyzing unique vocal tract characteristics rather than just the words spoken.

## 5. Next-Generation Customer Interaction
* **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Chatbot**: A natural language chat interface that translates user questions in plain English into backend queries to fetch contextual transaction data and financial insights.
* **Conversational AI with Emotion Detection**: An advanced assistant capable of detecting user frustration or panic and automatically routing them to a human agent if stress levels are high.
* **Hyper-Personalized Marketing Generation**: Uses Generative AI to create custom dashboard banners and email copy tailored specifically to what individual users value (e.g., travel rewards vs. cashback).

## 6. Smart Document Processing (OCR & Vision)
* **Auto-fill Receipts/Bills**: Allows users to upload a photo of a receipt or utility bill. Vision AI or OCR libraries extract the Amount, Date, and Merchant name to auto-populate data entry forms.
* **Automated KYC Verification**: Automatically extracts text and faces from uploaded ID cards and compares them against the user's selfie to generate a "Confidence Score," streamlining administrative reviews.

## 7. Credit & Lending Innovations
* **Alternative Credit Scoring**: Assesses creditworthiness for users without strong credit histories by analyzing alternative data such as utility payments, rent history, income deposit consistency, and merchant types.
* **Real-time Micro-Lending**: Pre-approves users for instant micro-loans at the point of sale if their account balance is too low, based on an AI assessment of their likelihood to repay from their next paycheck.

## 8. Administrative Operations (AIOps) & Compliance
* **Automated Regulatory Compliance**: NLP models constantly read new financial regulations and cross-reference them against internal policies to ensure automatic and continuous compliance.
* **Threat Intelligence**: AI models monitor admin logs to detect sudden spikes in failed login attempts, dictionary attacks, or strange API usage, elevating critical system alerts instantly.
* **Automated Chargeback Resolution**: Automatically gathers receipt data, location information, and merchant history to generate dispute packages, resolving simple claims instantly without human intervention.
